# Rex: Pursuit key art v2

Added to the portfolio on 2026-09-25. It replaces [v1](rex-pursuit-keyart-v1.md), whose green military Jeep did not match the game.

The source PNG is `rex-pursuit-keyart-v2.png` in this directory, an unmodified copy of `art/rex-pursuit-keyart-v3.png` in the Rex: Pursuit repository. That file's notes hold the generation prompts. It keeps the v1 composition, T. rex and golden-hour jungle, and repaints the vehicle as the game's khaki camouflage park Jeep with its red windshield frame, red stripes, number 18, roll cage and rear-mounted gun. The door badge is a plain dinosaur silhouette with no lettering.

This is promotional artwork, not a gameplay screenshot. The WebP variants in `assets/images/` are centre-cropped from 1.91:1 to 16:9 and resized without creative retouching: `rex-pursuit-keyart-v2-wide.webp` (1600x900) and `rex-pursuit-keyart-v2-card.webp` (720x405).
